Ramazan Cebimde, Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı'nın ya da başka bir kurumun resmî uygulaması değildir.

Prayer times, adhan alarms, a monthly timetable and the qibla direction in one app.

Get it on Google Play

Free for Android. Contains ads; ads can be removed with an optional purchase.

Features

  • Prayer times and a monthly timetable based on data from the Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet)
  • Adhan and reminder alarms, with separate notification settings for each prayer
  • Iftar and sahur countdown, Ramadan counter
  • Qibla direction
  • Tasbih counter and a calendar of religious days
  • A verse from the Quran every day
  • Home screen widget and colour themes
  • The whole app works without an account; sign in only if you want to back up your settings
  • Turkish and English
